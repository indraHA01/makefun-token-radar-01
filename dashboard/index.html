The Ignition model

A launch is an ignition event. The market has already told us what one is worth: live tokens trade at a price per dollar of TVL, and that price differs by sector. A dollar of bridge TVL is worth far more as a token than a dollar of liquid-staking TVL. So the model reads the snapshot's own live-token set, takes the median market-cap-per-TVL of each category, and prices every pre-token protocol at its own sector's multiple.

implied value = TVL × sector multiple × growth × maturity × breadth

  • Sector multiple — median mcap/TVL of the live tokens in the protocol's category (global median when a category has fewer than 3 live tokens). Clamped to 0.05–3.00.
  • Growth — 1 + clamped 7-day TVL change (−50% to +150%) × 0.6. A launch lands harder on a rising protocol.
  • Maturity — 0.7 + min(age, 1095 days) / 1095 × 0.3. Pent-up demand: the longer a protocol has run with big TVL and no token, the more expectation has built around its launch.
  • Breadth — 1 + min(chains − 1, 5) × 0.02. More chains, more surface to launch from.

Ignition score & urgency

Ignition (0–100) blends where a protocol stands on value (its rank percentile, 40%) with the heat of its factors — momentum 25%, maturity 20%, sector price 10%, breadth 5%. A high score means the launch window is hot. Urgency reads maturity against momentum: Farm now when the window looks narrow, Farm steadily in the middle, Early — build slowly while there is time to build a position.

Velocity

Implied value earned per day since listing. Sorting by velocity surfaces young giants — protocols that accumulated enormous implied value in very little time — before the plain TVL ranking does.

The snapshot is DefiLlama's protocol record as of the date above; it says a protocol records no token, never that it has none. An implied value is this entry's own claim about what a launch would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is — not a prediction that a token will be issued, or when. Farm plans are generic guidance, not financial advice; verify every step on the protocol's own pages before you move funds.
